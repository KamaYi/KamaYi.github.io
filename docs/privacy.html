Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Time Capsule ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

📋

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and password
  • Profile Information: Username and profile photo (optional)
  • User Content: Text, photos, and audio you create in capsules

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Features you use, app interactions
  • Location Data: Approximate location (if permitted)
⚙️

2. How We Use Your Information

  • ✔ Provide and maintain App functionality
  • ✔ Store and sync time capsules
  • ✔ Send notifications when capsules unlock
  • ✔ Respond to support requests
  • ✔ Improve user experience
  • ✔ Detect and prevent security issues
🔐

3. Data Storage and Security

🛡️ Data Encryption

Your data (including photos and wishes/messages) is stored with the following security measures:

In Transit

All data is encrypted using TLS/SSL during transmission between your device and our servers.

At Rest

Your capsule content (text, photos, audio) is stored on secure cloud servers provided by Supabase with server-side encryption enabled.

Access Control

Your data can only be accessed by your authenticated account. We use row-level security policies to ensure data isolation.

⚠️

Note

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

📦

4. Data Retention Policy

Sealed Capsules

Your capsule content is retained until the capsule unlocks and you choose to delete it, or until you delete your account.

Unlocked Capsules

After a capsule unlocks, content remains accessible until you manually delete it.

Account Data

Retained until you request account deletion.

Deleted Data

When you delete content or your account, data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

📱

5. App Permissions

🖼️

Photo Library Access

We request access to your Photo Library so you can select and add photos to your time capsules. We only access photos you explicitly select - we never scan or access your entire photo library.

🔔

Push Notifications

We use push notifications to alert you when your time capsules are ready to be opened. You can disable notifications in your settings at any time.

🔄

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We share your information with third-party service providers only to the extent necessary for them to provide services to us, such as data storage, authentication, and push notifications. These providers are obligated to protect your information and only use it as directed by us.

⚖️
